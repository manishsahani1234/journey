Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions before getting started? Here are answers to common client queries.

How does the 360° Digital Growth & Promotion Package work?
We handle your entire digital presence from scratch: we design your custom website or landing page, optimize and rank your Google Business Profile (Map/GMB) for local searches, design professional social media graphic banners, edit promotional video reels, write SEO blog articles, and provide backlink support to drive organic inquiries.
Can you build cross-platform mobile apps for both iOS and Android?
Yes! Using modern frameworks like Flutter and React Native along with Figma prototyping, we build high-performance mobile apps with a single codebase that runs smoothly on both iPhones (iOS) and Android smartphones, complete with push notifications and backend database connection.
I already have a website that is slow or has bugs. Can you fix and maintain it?
Yes! Our Website Care Retainer is specially designed for businesses that already have a website or app. We optimize loading speeds to 90+ on Google PageSpeed, remove malware, perform daily backups, resolve bugs, and keep your plugins and servers secure every month.
Will I be able to edit my website content easily without coding?
Yes! All WordPress websites I build feature intuitive admin panels using Elementor or custom Gutenberg blocks so you can easily update text, images, products, and pages without any technical knowledge.
